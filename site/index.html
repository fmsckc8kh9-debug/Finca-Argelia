Our Coffee

Traditional Bourbon, Perfected Over Generations

40-year-old Bourbon trees, their roots deep in volcanic clay, shaded by Cedro and Gravilea, fed by three natural springs. Fully washed at our on-farm wet mill. SCA 83 points.

Tasting profile: chocolate, brown sugar, soft citrus, mild stone fruit, smooth body, clean finish.

Conservation

450+ Hectares of Protected Forest

In partnership with INAB, Guatemala's National Forestry Institute, Finca Argelia maintains the largest private protected forest in the region. Over 450 hectares of native forest stand alongside our 70 hectares of coffee.

Since 2023, we've been actively replanting 45 hectares per year with native species — rebuilding what time and previous land use had taken. Three natural springs fed by the protected watershed sustain the farm and the surrounding community.
